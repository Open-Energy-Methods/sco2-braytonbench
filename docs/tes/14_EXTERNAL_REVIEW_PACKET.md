An AI-assisted authoring process can prepare tests, check algebra and organize evidence. **It cannot stand in for an independent qualified human reviewer**, determine legal rights, or truthfully sign on behalf of a scientist.

## A. Reviewer roles and independence

At least two actual competencies are needed, which may come from qualified individuals or an appropriately qualified team:
- **Thermodynamics/numerical methods:** conservation laws, fixed/variable heat transfer closure, spatial/time refinement, thermal storage and physically interpretable exergy/grade metrics.
- **Scientific software/reproducibility:** Python packaging and support, test provenance, controlled numerical fixtures, deterministic/trusted ML evaluation, archive rights exclusions and source-integrity process.

The reviewer(s) must be independent of the code author and AI implementation. Potential conflicts of interest, prior contributions, affiliation and specific review capacity must be declared. A pull-request approval without actual supporting review findings does not establish an independent scientific derivation.
